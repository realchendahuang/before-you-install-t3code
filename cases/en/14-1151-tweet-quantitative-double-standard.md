> Exhaustive quantitative analysis of 1,151 consecutive @theo X posts (Nov 7–24, 2026) proves a stark power asymmetry: 0.0% profanity toward 20 big-tech executives and lab staff vs. 8.0% hostility toward ordinary developers — surging 4.0× to 14.4% during the 25-hour Chinese-fork dispute.

## What the Evidence Shows

Analyzing every consecutive `@theo` X post from **November 7–24, 2026** (`n = 1,151`; `17.24` days; `66.8` posts/day; `75.6%` replies; `2.7`-minute median interval) establishes a measurable, reproducible hierarchy of respect:

- **Big-Tech Executives, Lab Staff & Top KOLs (`n = 43` strict / `60`–`62` broader):** **0.0%** targeted profanity or personal insults (`30.0%`–`32.6%` polite thanks). Even critiques of major labs (Gemini 4 Carbon, GPT-6.2 Astra) were softened with praise or followed by public apologies ([`2123255588570501585`](https://x.com/theo/status/2123255588570501585), [`2123995323123343869`](https://x.com/theo/status/2123995323123343869)).
- **Ordinary Developers & Critics (`n = 766` strict / `749` broader):** Hostility and profanity hit **8.0%** (`61/766`) — including quote-tweeting a basic tool comparison to declare *"Engineers deserve the mass unemployment they have coming"* ([`2124470530590011704`](https://x.com/theo/status/2124470530590011704)), wanting to *"dance on the graves of the 'local ai' community"* ([`2120733839832273025`](https://x.com/theo/status/2120733839832273025)), calling Linux users *"Linux larpers"* ([`2124298216271499605`](https://x.com/theo/status/2124298216271499605)), repeated `"stupid bitch"` attacks ([`2120599310211703274`](https://x.com/theo/status/2120599310211703274)), and jail/RSD mockery ([`2122865897375400066`](https://x.com/theo/status/2122865897375400066)).
- **25-Hour Chinese-Fork Crisis Window (`n = 132`):** Direct personal insults surged **4.0×** from the `3.6%` baseline to **14.4%** (`19/132`), including calling Chinese tech Twitter *"the biggest fucking cesspit"* and labeling developers reading Chinese code audits *"fucking braindead"* ([`2125177503526588877`](https://x.com/theo/status/2125177503526588877)).
