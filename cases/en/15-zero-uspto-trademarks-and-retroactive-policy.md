# #15 · Zero USPTO Trademarks Behind the GitHub Ban Threat & 17-Hour Retroactive `TRADEMARK.md`

> When @theo threatened to contact GitHub to take down a Chinese developer's account for "trademark infringement", T3 Tools Inc. held 0 USPTO registrations for `T3 Code`, `T3 Tools`, or `T3 Connect` — and retroactively merged `TRADEMARK.md` 17 hours later.

<!-- section:claim -->
## What Was Claimed

- On November 23, `@theo` accused the MIT fork author of trademark infringement and threatened a GitHub account takedown ([`2124829315657576890`](https://x.com/theo/status/2124829315657576890)).
- Three hours before merging `TRADEMARK.md`, he told questioning developers to *"get a better lawyer"* ([`2125026423769555308`](https://x.com/theo/status/2125026423769555308)) — despite having mocked WordPress in September 2024 over an *"unenforceable trademark dispute"* ([`1839261574005141945`](https://x.com/theo/status/1839261574005141945)).

<!-- section:evidence -->
## What the Evidence Shows

- **Zero USPTO registrations for T3 marks:** Direct USPTO TSDR verification (following a lead from `@vildanden_ai`, [`2125275487404761555`](https://x.com/vildanden_ai/status/2125275487404761555)) shows **0 registrations** for `T3 Code`, `T3 Tools`, or `T3 Connect`. T3 Tools Inc.'s sole USPTO filing (`PING LABS`, Serial [`97294243`](https://tsdr.uspto.gov/statusview/sn97294243)) was refused in February 2025 and marked `ABANDONED` on May 19, 2025. Meanwhile, unrelated third parties hold active `T3` software/SaaS registrations in Classes 9 and 42 (`EquipmentShare`, Reg. `7795672` / Serial [`90644500`](https://tsdr.uspto.gov/#caseNumber=90644500&caseSearchType=US_APPLICATION&caseType=DEFAULT&searchType=statusSearch); `ESG Operations`, Reg. `3324707`).
- **GitHub Trademark Policy requirement:** Under [GitHub's Trademark Policy](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/content-removal-policies/github-trademark-policy), commercial trademark takedown requests require a federal or international registration number under penalty of perjury. At the moment `@theo` threatened to take down `@DDdxx666`'s account, he could not complete GitHub's form.
- **17-hour retroactive `TRADEMARK.md` (`PR #41898`):** At **12:21 CST on November 24** — 17 hours after the ban threat — `@theo` merged [`PR #41898`](https://github.com/pingdotgg/t3code/pull/41898) inserting `TRADEMARK.md` claiming rights *"whether registered or unregistered"*, and cited it on X 94 seconds later ([`2125082769395220688`](https://x.com/theo/status/2125082769395220688)).
