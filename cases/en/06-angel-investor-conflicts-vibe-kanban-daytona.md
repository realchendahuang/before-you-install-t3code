# #06 · Angel-Investor Conflicts: Vibe Kanban Shutdown Mockery & Daytona Overlap

> Beyond code defects, @theo's public X posts document sharp conflicts between his role as an angel investor in competing developer startups and his operation of T3 Code.

<!-- section:claim -->
## What Was Claimed

- `@theo` routinely presents himself as an independent developer advocate sharing candid, unbiased takes on coding tools and startup workflows.

<!-- section:evidence -->
## What the Evidence Shows

- **Vibe Kanban investment & public mockery (`2122201981167251877`):** On November 16, `@theo` publicly disclosed that he had been an angel investor and contributor to **Vibe Kanban**. He recounted that the team pivoted away from kanban toward T3 Code's UI pattern before running out of runway and shutting down — then publicly mocked that UI as great *"for running out of runway and killing your business."*
- **Daytona investment alongside T3 paid sandboxes (`2119705561679933806` → `2123011578090127499`):** Nine days after floating paid cloud sandboxes inside T3 Code (Nov 9), `@theo` publicly confirmed on November 18 that he is an investor in **Daytona**, a cloud sandbox infrastructure startup.
- **Broader unflagged overlaps:** Public records also confirm `@theo` holds angel investments in competing AI coding tools **Cursor** and **Cline** while actively publishing product critiques and traffic-driving comparisons across the same market.
