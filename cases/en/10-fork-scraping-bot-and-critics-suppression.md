# #10 · Public `@bot` Fork-Scraping, Competitor Mockery & Critic Suppression

> While mocking rivals for copying UI patterns, @theo publicly replied "Okay I'm stealing this" to a developer's fork and dispatched `@bot` 30 seconds later to scrape her code — while deploying auto-blockers and urging followers to demonetize critics.

<!-- section:claim -->
## What Was Claimed

- On November 24, `@theo` publicly mocked Claude Code Desktop for adding sidebar tabs ([`2124987957803446311`](https://x.com/theo/status/2124987957803446311)) and portrayed himself as defending original product work against knockoffs.

<!-- section:evidence -->
## What the Evidence Shows

- **Public `@bot` fork-scraping in 30 seconds (`2119240810202034645`):** On November 8, after independent developer `@MiaAI_lab` shared a custom fork feature she built, `@theo` replied *"Okay I'm stealing this"* and 30 seconds later publicly tagged his own automation bot (`@bot`) in the replies to locate and scrape her fork onto `beebox` using Opus 5.5.
- **Cloning rival features on demand (`2121017649652269120`):** On November 13, when a user praised features in competing tools, `@theo` again dispatched `@bot` in public replies to clone those capabilities directly into T3 Code.
- **Demonetizing critics & automated mass blocking:** Against critics on X, `@theo` encouraged followers to file Community Notes specifically to *"demonetize them"* ([`2120238923884696025`](https://x.com/theo/status/2120238923884696025)), and publicly confirmed operating an automated X blocker bot that blocked roughly 100 accounts on November 24 alone ([`2125077510941802509`](https://x.com/theo/status/2125077510941802509)).
