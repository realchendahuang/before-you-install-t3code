# #00 · Origin Dispute: "Steal Our Code", Fork Naming Threats & Racial Double Standards

> Within 11 minutes, @theo labeled a Chinese developer's free MIT fork a "Chinese knockoff" and "Temu3 Code" and threatened a GitHub account ban — then publicly welcomed trillion-dollar US companies copying MIT code less than 11 hours later.

<!-- section:claim -->
## What Was Claimed

- T3 Code's homepage ([`t3.codes`](https://t3.codes/)) labeled its public GitHub link *"Steal our code (legally)"* under an MIT license.
- Less than 11 hours after attacking a Chinese developer's MIT fork, asked whether trillion-dollar US companies copying T3 Code bothered him, `@theo` replied: *"Nah. Open source is open source, even if a trillion dollar company uses it!"* ([`2124989435154726982`](https://x.com/theo/status/2124989435154726982)).

<!-- section:evidence -->
## What the Evidence Shows

- **Targeted hostility over an MIT fork (Nov 23, 19:11–19:54 CST):** After a bystander mistook a free MIT fork by Chinese developer `@DDdxx666` for the official app, `@theo` posted twice within 11 minutes calling it a *"Chinese knockoff"* and *"Temu3 Code"* and threatened to have the developer's entire GitHub account removed: *"Do it right now or I'm going to contact GitHub and have your account taken down"* ([`2124829315657576890`](https://x.com/theo/status/2124829315657576890)).
- **Immediate compliance, asymmetric apology:** `@DDdxx666` renamed the repository to `Stardust` and removed all T3 branding within 30 minutes. `@theo` apologized privately in direct messages, while leaving both public posts (over 200,000 combined views) live.
- **Selective enforcement by nationality:** T3 team member `@maria_rcks` publicly hosted `maria-rcks/t3libre` (*"T3 Code, liberated"*), and English-language repositories such as `t3code-quota-ui`, `t3code-atlas`, and `t3code-bar` operated undisturbed with zero takedown threats.
- **Own inspiration acknowledged in FAQ:** T3 Code's own FAQ explicitly states it drew direct inspiration from Codex Desktop, Conductor, Claude Desktop, and Cursor ([`2125066677188702698`](https://x.com/realchendahuang/status/2125066677188702698)).
- **Catalyst for the full investigation:** Starting November 24, line-by-line review of `pingdotgg/t3code` and public records uncovered the 19 documented exhibits that follow — prompting a European GDPR complaint and nearly 20 emergency overnight patches.

<!-- section:status -->
## Upstream Status (`ca81b7b9`)

- **Public record:** Both November 23 public attacks and the November 24 *"Open source is open source"* reply remain part of the permanent public record.
