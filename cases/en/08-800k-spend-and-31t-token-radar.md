# #08 · The $800,000+ Business Ledger & 31.7-Trillion-Token Daily Compute Radar

> Default-on telemetry was not an engineering oversight: it financed a real-time 31.7T-token/day ($10.19M/day equivalent API spend) intelligence radar traded for $300k+ in free OpenAI early-access tokens and investor outreach.

<!-- section:claim -->
## What Was Claimed

- T3 Code was framed as a pure-passion free MIT open-source tool, and telemetry was defended merely as basic counting to *"know how many users we have."*
- `@theo` also claimed DeepMind's `@PatrickToulme` had landed *"many PRs"* into T3 Code.

<!-- section:evidence -->
## What the Evidence Shows

- **$800k+ spend & total pivot away from paid products:** Across all **1,151 consecutive X posts** from Nov 7–24, `@theo` mentioned T3 Code **162 times (14.1%)** versus **0 mentions** of his legacy paid products (`T3 Chat`, `UploadThing`, `ping.gg`), while publicly confirming **$800k+** in out-of-pocket spend on the free client ([`2120729779511124267`](https://x.com/theo/status/2120729779511124267)).
- **31.7T-token/day ($10.19M/day) analytics radar:** Raw events retained through closed-source `ingest.t3.codes` powered an internal dashboard tracking **31.7 trillion tokens/day** (**$10.19 million/day** equivalent API spend on Nov 18, peaking near **$10.2M/day**) and fine-grained lab share splits (`99%` subscription-backed, `~98.5%` across Codex/Claude/Antigravity, open-weight share falling from `15%` to `6%`). When a commenter speculated on market shares on Nov 23, `@theo` mocked them for lacking access to his analytics ([`2124682906132181482`](https://x.com/theo/status/2124682906132181482)).
- **Converting user telemetry into lab perks & VC leverage:** `@theo` publicly noted burning **$300k+ in unlimited free OpenAI early-access tokens** on a GPT-6.2 Astra benchmark ([`2122760447103484410`](https://x.com/theo/status/2122760447103484410)), running Google bare-metal benchmarks, and fielding investor outreach (*"multiple investors have asked me about this"*, [`2123885732330885417`](https://x.com/theo/status/2123885732330885417)).
- **Exaggerated lab collaboration:** Git commit history filtered by `patrick-toulme` shows exactly **1 commit** ([`PR #31158`](https://github.com/pingdotgg/t3code/pull/31158)), not *"many PRs."*

<!-- section:status -->
## Upstream Status (`ca81b7b9`)

- **Public record / active business model:** `ingest.t3.codes` remains closed-source and default-enabled at `ca81b7b9`, continuing to feed the same first-party analytics pipeline.
