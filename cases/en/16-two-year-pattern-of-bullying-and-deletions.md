# #16 · Two-Year Historical Pattern: Nationality Backlashes, $20k FFmpeg Bounty & Deleted Threats

> The November 2026 Chinese-fork outburst was not an isolated lapse — public records from 2024 to 2026 document a repeating cycle of nationality-targeted harassment, open-source bullying, mocking other developers' UIs, and post-backlash deletions.

<!-- section:claim -->
## What Was Claimed

- In his 2024 apology video [`I screwed up.`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=o2CSJYXJBpc), `@theo` explicitly condemned directing nationality-based hostility at developer communities.

<!-- section:evidence -->
## What the Evidence Shows

- **2024 Indian developer harassment & apology:** After publishing a video targeting Indian programming tutorial channels that triggered waves of nationality-based harassment, `@theo` uploaded [`I screwed up.`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=o2CSJYXJBpc) condemning such attacks — two years before calling a Chinese MIT fork `"Temu3 Code"` and labeling Chinese tech Twitter *"the biggest fucking cesspit."*
- **2024 fork mockery & mocking DHH's UI:** `@theo` previously named his own fork of another developer's VS Code theme [`vsc-material-but-i-wont-sue-you`](https://github.com/t3dotgg/vsc-material-but-i-wont-sue-you) to mock the original author's legal sensitivity, and built viral engagement in June 2024 publicly ridiculing Rails creator DHH's email client UI ([`2125066552785711291`](https://x.com/realchendahuang/status/2125066552785711291)).
- **Oct 2025 — $20,000 FFmpeg bounty:** During an X dispute with FFmpeg, `@theo` publicly offered a **$20,000 bounty** to push out an FFmpeg maintainer ([documented record](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1oQ3KMeaH04)). After Windscribe counter-offered $21,000, VLC's founder opposed him, and FFmpeg developers documented the harassment, `@theo` withdrew the bounty and deleted the video and posts.
- **Stop Killing Games backlash & prior YouTube documentation:** Earlier consumer-rights disputes saw `@theo` insult *Stop Killing Games* advocates repeatedly while siding with publishers ([`2125064674307985520`](https://x.com/realchendahuang/status/2125064674307985520)), alongside long-form YouTube documentation comparing his public statements against private messages ([`2125066387647513072`](https://x.com/realchendahuang/status/2125066387647513072)).
- **Nov 25, 2026 (~4:00 AM) — deleted closed-source threat:** During the overnight audit fallout, `@theo` posted a threat to take T3 Code closed-source because users did not deserve open source — then deleted the post before sunrise after it crossed 3,000 likes.
