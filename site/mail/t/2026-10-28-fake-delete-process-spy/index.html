Users documented two local storage and privacy defects across Issues #17845, #28903, and #11222:

  1. Fake permanent delete (#17845): The confirmation dialog promised "This permanently clears conversation history for this thread", yet ThreadDeletion.ts only appended a thread.deleted event — bloating statev2.sqlite to 25.9 GB in 3 weeks and 41.6 GB in 6 weeks.
  2. 5-second OS process scan (#11222 & PR #41889): Native helper t3-resource-monitor ran ProcessesToUpdate::All with with_cmd(UpdateKind::Always) every 5 seconds even when diagnostics were closed, sweeping 440+ unrelated apps and games along with full command lines (which often hold plaintext API keys and passwords).
